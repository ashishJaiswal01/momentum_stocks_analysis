Institutional Momentum Screening & Execution — Strategy 2

A stricter, institutional-grade momentum screen. Before scoring any stock, two non-negotiable gates apply: a macro regime gate (Nifty 500 must be above its 200-day SMA with the 50-day SMA at or above the 200-day SMA, or all new BUY entries halt system-wide) and a liquidity gate (90-day average daily traded value ≥ ₹5 Cr and market cap ≥ ₹2,000 Cr). Surviving stocks are then scored on three pillars: 52-week-high anchor + trend (within 10% of the 52-week high, trading above both EMA50 and EMA200), earnings quality (EPS near its 3-year peak or growing ≥15% YoY, operating cash flow covering reported profit, 3-year ROE ≥12% with controlled leverage), and dual relative strength (12-month return skipping the most recent month, beating both the Nifty 500 and the stock's sector). All 3 pillars plus both gates open -> SUPER_PERFORMER (Tier 1, equal-weighted 5-7% position, stop at max(EMA50, entry − 3×ATR14)); Pillar 1 + Pillar 2 only -> PERFORMER (Tier 2, hold); anything else (Pillar 1 broken, cash-flow test failed, or liquidity no longer sufficient) is dropped from the scan entirely, regardless of prior tracking. Intended for monthly/bi-weekly rebalancing, not daily trading.
